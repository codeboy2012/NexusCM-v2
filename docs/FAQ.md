# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is NexusCM?

A modern self-hosted Matrix platform.

---

## Is NexusCM open source?

Yes.

---

## What client does it use?

Cinny.

---

## Can I use another Matrix client?

Yes.

NexusCM follows the Matrix specification.

---

## Does it federate?

Yes.

Federation is supported through Synapse.

---

## Is Docker required?

Docker is the recommended deployment method.

---

## Can I contribute?

Absolutely.

Contributions are welcome.

---

## Is NexusCM production ready?

Not yet.

NexusCM v2 is currently under active development.
